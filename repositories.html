Software & code

Open-source R packages and Shiny apps I’ve built for analyzing Drosophila sleep and circadian rhythms, general-purpose visualization and UI tools for ggplot2 and Shiny, and side projects in typeface design and generative graphics. Find my profile and personal repositories on github.com/orijitghosh.

  • FlyDreamR

    An R package to infer sleep and wake states from locomotor activity data using hidden Markov models, accompanying our 2026 bioRxiv preprint on the genetic basis of sleep states in Drosophila melanogaster. Maintained under NHLBI/FlyDreamR (personal fork at orijitghosh/FlyDreamR).

  • VANESSA-DAM

    Visualization and ANalysis of timE SerieS dAta — Drosophila Activity Monitors. A suite of interactive Shiny apps for high-throughput circadian rhythm and sleep time-series analysis.

    Paper

  • ggsketch

    Grammar-native, hand-drawn geoms for ggplot2: the rough.js sketch aesthetic in pure R with no JavaScript or browser. Works with aes(), stats, scales, and facets across every graphics device, including PDF.

    CRAN

  • shadshiny

    A from-scratch port of the shadcn/ui design system to R Shiny: 73 accessible, themeable components written in framework-free CSS and vanilla JavaScript, wrapped as ordinary R functions with native Shiny input bindings.
